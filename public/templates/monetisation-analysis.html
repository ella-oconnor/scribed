Example — Beacon
  1. Enterprise customer support AI is a $12B market growing 24%/year. SMB chat widgets are a $3B market with declining margins.
  2. Start with SMBs at $49–$199/mo to prove the product. Intercom did this — started with startup chat, now sells $200K enterprise deals.
  3. Exit multiples are 8–15x ARR for enterprise platforms. At $10M ARR, Beacon could be worth $80–$150M.
  4. Enterprise buyers pilot for 3 months, then sign annual contracts. Land-and-expand is the motion — start with one team, spread to the org.
  5. SMB-only companies stall. Zendesk went public but flatlined at 5x ARR because SMBs churn at 5–8% monthly.
