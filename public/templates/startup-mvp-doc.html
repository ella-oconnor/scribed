How to use this template

This document is a complete MVP product framework. It covers the 10 sections that investors, co-founders, and teams need to take your idea seriously.

  • Prompts (dashed boxes) tell you what to write. Delete them once you've filled in your content.
  • Examples (shaded boxes labelled "Example") show what good looks like using a fictional company called Glassbox — an AI contract review tool. Use them as a reference, then delete.
  • Every section includes strategic guidance — not just "insert text here."
  • Spend 2–4 hours filling this in. If a section takes longer than 30 minutes, your thinking isn't clear enough yet — simplify.

Example — Glassbox
Core insight

Contract risk is invisible until it's catastrophic. A bad clause costs nothing on the day you sign it. It costs everything on the day it triggers. That's why people skip reviews — the pain is deferred, so the urgency is zero. Glassbox makes the risk visible before you sign.

  • Fundraising — "My SAFE has clauses I don't fully understand and I don't want to look stupid asking my investor to explain."
  • Enterprise customer — "A big customer sent their MSA. It's 40 pages. I have no idea what I'm agreeing to."

Glassbox is AI contract review that finds the clauses lawyers miss — in 90 seconds, not 3 billable hours.

The customer is a startup founder or ops lead who signs 10–30 contracts per quarter without in-house legal.

The moat is a clause-level risk database trained on 50,000 real contract disputes — not a generic language model.

Growth comes from 100 founders who love it so much they forward the report to their lawyer and say "why didn't you catch this?"

The single question: does Glassbox consistently find a clause the user missed?
