How to use this template

A structured framework for making career decisions based on evidence rather than instinct. Use it when choosing between job offers, deciding whether to stay or leave, or comparing 2–4 career paths.

  • Fill it in once, then revisit and re-score after every conversation, interview, or new piece of information.
  • Examples use a fictional graduate called Alex choosing between a return offer at an ad agency vs a strategy role at a bank in Edinburgh. Delete after use.
  • Be honest. This document is for you — not your parents, not LinkedIn, not your interviewer.
  • Takes 30–60 minutes to fill in properly. If you can't score a criterion, you don't have enough information yet — that's useful data.

Rule of thumb

Don't stack more than one big bet in the same month. Moving city + starting a new job + finishing a thesis = burnout. Sequence where possible.

5. The hard questions

These are the questions you need to answer honestly before committing. If you can't answer them, you need more information — go have more conversations.

Customise these to your situation. The best decision questions are specific, uncomfortable, and force you to confront trade-offs.
  1. If I choose [Path A], what specifically am I giving up that I can't get back? [Answer]
  2. If I choose [Path B], what's the worst realistic outcome in 12 months? [Answer]
  3. What would "success" look like in 36 months in each path? (Title, skills, city, network, savings) [Answer]
  4. Am I choosing this because I want it, or because it sounds impressive? [Answer]
  5. Which path has the best "option value" — i.e. keeps the most doors open for future moves? [Answer]
Example — Alex
  1. If I return to the agency, I give up Edinburgh and proximity to my partner for at least another year. That's real.
  2. If I go to the bank, worst case: I hate the structure, the work is dry, and I'm job-hunting again in 18 months. But I'd be in Edinburgh with a strong CV line.
  3. 36-month success at agency: Senior creative strategist, deep brand expertise, London-based, £58K. At bank: Associate, breadth across markets, Edinburgh-based, £52K + bonus.
  4. Honestly? The bank sounds more impressive to other people. The agency is what I actually enjoy day-to-day.
